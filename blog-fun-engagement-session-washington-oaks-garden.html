Engagement Photography in Palm Coast

This beautiful couple could not have chosen a better location for their engagement session. Washington Oaks Gardens is a beautiful place for engagement photography in Palm Coast. We were all stunned by just how gorgeous of an afternoon it was and how thankful we were to be able to capture it all!

We moved through the different sections of the park on a quiet afternoon. It is rare to be able to get so much of the gardens to yourself, but we had the timing just right. We could take it slow and draw out some beautiful images for them. Even the pup was in the mood for photos, and we were able to get a bunch of beautiful family portraits.

We love having the opportunity to capture these timeless moments for couples as they begin the journey of a lifetime together. Thank you for working with us and allowing us the opportunity to serve you!
