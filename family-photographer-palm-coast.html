On The Day

Nervous, wiggly or not into it. Everyone is welcome.

Every family shows up with someone who would rather be anywhere else. The secret is talking to each person about what worries them, and telling them how I will help.

The kidsI promise them the silly photos, in between or at the end. The Spiderman pose, the gymnastics moves. Knowing their silly side gets its turn too makes them excited to give those smiles to grandma.
The grownupsYou don't have to worry about a thing. I guide you through every moment: stand here, try this. Nobody has to come up with anything, and nobody gets blamed for how a picture looks.

Good To Know

Questions families ask.

What time of day is best in summer?

Very early morning or late evening. Florida summers are hot, and those hours bring fresher air, softer light and happier kids.

My kids won't sit still. Is that a problem?

Not at all. I tell them that once we finish a few photos, we will do the silly ones: the Spiderman pose, the cartwheel, whatever they want to show me. Knowing their turn is coming makes them much more willing to smile for grandma.

My teenager really doesn't want to do this.

That's normal, and I don't blame them. I take a minute with them on their own, promise it will be quick and easy, and remind them how much it means to mom and grandma. It softens things up, every time.

We don't know how to pose.

You don't have to. I guide you through every moment, so you can relax and just be together.

What should we wear?

I wrote a whole guide for that: what to wear for family portraits.

Can we do our session at home?

Yes. Some of my favorite family and newborn sessions happen at home, on the sofa, in the nursery, with the dog and the cat included.
