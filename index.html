Documentary at Heart

The love unfolding all around you.

On your wedding day there is so much happening all around you that you'll never get to see. The way your mom looks at you. The way grandma holds grandpa's hand a little tighter. Your best friend crying behind you.

“You become a witness. You're not altering the scene. You're capturing all these moments they don't get to see, that become the ultimate treasures.”

It's why I never really stop moving through your day, and why a second photographer joins us when the day calls for it, so the gallery you receive holds hundreds of moments you didn't know happened.

A Little Magic

Something real to hold.

We live so much of our lives through a screen, and your wedding shouldn't only live there too. Your full gallery is crafted with care and worth the wait, but the feeling of your day is something I want you to be able to hold, not just scroll past.

“There's a moment where you get to look at your day, hold it in your hands, and think: wow, this really happened. That feeling is everything to me.”

So there may be a little something waiting for you, one of the many ways you'll get to feel your wedding all over again.
