Good To Know

Questions couples ask.

How many photos will we receive?

I shoot thousands of frames at your wedding, hunting for the best light and moments, then hand-select and fully edit the very best into your private gallery. As a guide, that's roughly 400+ images for a four-hour celebration, 600+ for six hours, and 900+ or well beyond for a full eight-hour day, every one beautifully finished. I deliver well over a hundred images an hour and have a gentle habit of over-delivering.

Do we get a second photographer?

A second photographer can be added to any collection for $500. It's worth it when your guest count passes about 75, or when you and your partner are getting ready in separate places, so both the vows and the tears in the front row are caught at the same time.

Do you travel for weddings?

Always. Beyond St. Augustine and Palm Coast, I've photographed weddings in Colombia, Puerto Rico, and across the country. Destination collections are custom, so reach out with your location and dates.

When do we get our photos?

You'll have a sneak peek within 72 hours, with your full gallery, print rights included, delivered in four to six weeks. The Full Day includes priority delivery.

Can we order albums and prints?

Yes. Your gallery is connected to a professional print shop for heirloom-quality prints, and custom albums are available so your day lives in your hands, not just on a screen.

How do we hold our date?

Dates book on a signed agreement and a retainer. Once your date is reserved, it's yours. Use "Check Your Date" and I'll be in touch personally.
