Clients Who Became Family

It starts as a booking. It rarely ends as one.

Some of the people I am closest to today, I met because they hired me. It might start with an engagement or a wedding, and somewhere between the sessions and the years after, the line between client and friend just quietly disappears.

“People who started as clients are now some of my closest friends. And it almost always begins with their engagement photos.”

So when they come back, a wedding date, then a pregnancy, then a new baby, and then another, it never feels like a new job. It feels like being let back into a story I have been part of all along.

Since Becoming A Mother

Now I see the moments only a mom knows to look for.

Having my own daughter, Lily, changed the way I photograph families. I catch the small things I used to walk past, the way a hand reaches out, the look that passes between a parent and a child when they think no one is watching.

“After you have been a mom, hearing a mother say I can’t remember when she was this small feels visceral. You understand that love. And the real gift is having your whole family in a beautiful picture, with you in it too.”

So I photograph the connection between parents and kids on purpose now, the ordinary, tender moments that we mothers so rarely get to stand outside of and actually see.

The Tender Ones

The soft moments, the ones in between.

Not every family photo is everyone lined up and grinning. Some of the ones you will hold onto longest are the quiet ones, a kiss on the beach, a little one lifted into the air, the calm right before everything changes. Those are the frames I look for.

Give it a few years and this is the photograph that stops you: look how small they were, look how gentle it all felt, right here.

The little ones who grow up in front of my lens, and the joy that never poses on cue.

A whole line of little ones, hand in hand down the sand.
