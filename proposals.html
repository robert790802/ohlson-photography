Proposals & Engagements

Before the wedding, there's the moment you ask.

The nervous walk. The shaking hands. The word yes, and everything after. I capture the very beginning of your story, on the beach, in the old streets of St. Augustine, wherever you choose to change your life.

The Surprise

I'm already there when you drop to one knee.

You plan it with me in secret. Then I hide in plain sight and catch the real thing, the nerves, the turn, the hand over the mouth, the happy tears. I even arrive with the champagne, chilled, so your very first toast is ready the moment you say yes.

The Grand Kind

With everyone hiding to celebrate.

Some couples want the whole family in on it. I keep them tucked out of sight until the yes, then they come running with rose petals in the air. You get the question, the tears, and the people who love you most, all in one gallery.

Or The Quiet Kind

Just the two of you, beneath the oaks.

Others want it private. No audience, no signs, just a beautiful spot and each other. I stay far back with a long lens so the moment stays entirely yours, and you barely know I'm there.

In The Heart Of St. Augustine

The Lightner, the bridge, the old city.

The coquina bridge at the Lightner Museum, the fort, the cobblestone streets. Couples travel from all over to propose in one of the most romantic towns in the country, and I know every corner and the best light in each.

The Whole Story

Leo & Veronica.

I was there when Leo asked, at the fountain in St. Augustine. I was there again for their engagement session on the beach. And I was there for the day it was all leading to. A proposal becomes an engagement, an engagement becomes a wedding, and I get to witness every chapter. The beginning really does have a way of becoming everything.
