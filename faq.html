Questions, Answered

Everything you are wondering,
before you even ask.

If your question is not here, just reach out. There is no such thing as a silly one, and I would rather you feel completely sure before your day.

Booking & Planning

How far in advance should we book you?

The most-loved dates go first, so most couples reach out nine to eighteen months ahead, and popular spring and fall Saturdays can book even earlier. That said, if your date is closer than that, always ask, sometimes the timing works out beautifully.

How do we reserve our date?

Your date is held once we have a signed agreement and your 25% retainer. Until then it stays open to anyone, so if you know, it is worth locking in early.

We are not sure how many hours we need. Can you help?

Absolutely. Tell me how your day is shaped, the getting ready, the ceremony, the party, and I will help you land on the coverage that captures all of it without paying for hours you do not need.

Do you help us build our wedding-day timeline?

Yes, and it is one of the most valuable things I do. The number one thing is making sure you feel reassured that someone is looking out for you and guiding you through every part of the day, so the photography actually works and you stay present.

My Style & Approach

What is your photography style?

Documentary at heart. I am a witness to your day, catching the real, unscripted moments most people never get to see, the way your mom looks at you, your best friend crying behind you. When you need direction, I gently guide you, but I never turn your wedding into a stiff photoshoot.

We are awkward in front of a camera. What then?

Nearly everyone feels that way, and that is exactly where I come in. I give you a few simple, natural prompts that make you feel at ease, and within minutes most couples forget the camera is even there.

Is a second photographer included?

A second photographer is available for any wedding as a $500 add-on. There is only one of me and often hundreds of guests, so a second shooter helps make sure the moments happening in two places at once, and all those reactions, are never missed. It is an especially good idea once your guest count passes about 75, or when you and your partner are getting ready in separate places.

¿Hablas español?

Sí. I am fully bilingual in English and Spanish, and it means the world to my Spanish-speaking families to feel completely at home on their day.

The Wedding Day

What happens if it rains?

Florida weather can surprise anyone, so we plan for it from the start. The timeline does not stop because of rain, and I come prepared to keep shooting and still create beautiful, magical images. Some of the most stunning photographs happen in exactly the weather nobody wished for.

How do family photos work?

Family portraits are the part everyone dreads, so I keep them organized and quick. Send me your list ahead of time and we move through it painlessly, then get you back to your celebration.

Will you take a break during the reception?

I am famously bad at sitting still, because the moments are always happening. I stay ready and keep my eyes open all night, which is exactly how I catch the moments you will treasure most.

After The Day

When will we see our photos?

You will get a sneak peek of a handful of favorites within 72 hours, something to relive the day while the full gallery is crafted. Your complete, carefully edited gallery, with full printing rights included, follows within four to six weeks.

How many photos will we receive?

It scales with your coverage, more hours means more of your story told. Rather than a set number, the goal is every moment that matters, curated to the very best frames, with nothing meaningful left out.

Do we get to print our photos?

Yes. Your gallery comes with the ability to download and print your images, because the ultimate reward is having these photos in your hands, to hold, to frame, to hang on your walls.

Do you offer albums and prints?

I do, and I believe in them deeply. A printed album is how your day lives on for the next forty years, not just on a drive, but something your grandchildren can hold.

Travel & Destinations

Do you travel for weddings?

Yes, gladly. Being invited to photograph a wedding somewhere new is another level of love, and blending my passion for travel with photography is a gift. Wherever your love takes you, I would be honored to come along.

Are there travel fees?

For weddings beyond the local area, travel is quoted simply and transparently as part of your collection, so there are never any surprises.

Booking Terms

What are your payment terms?

A 25% non-refundable retainer reserves your date, with the balance due two weeks before your wedding day. We keep it simple and clear, and I am always happy to walk you through it.

Do you offer payment plans?

Yes. Your balance can be spread across comfortable payments leading up to the wedding, so it never lands all at once.
