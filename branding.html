Good To Know

Questions before we create.

What is the difference between a headshot and a branding session?

A headshot gives you one polished, professional portrait for LinkedIn, your website, press or a company profile. A branding session goes further. We create a complete library of portraits, working images, details, products and real moments that show people who you are, what you do and what it feels like to work with you.

Do you travel to St. Augustine, Ponte Vedra or Jacksonville for branding sessions?

Absolutely. I photograph businesses on location throughout Palm Coast, St. Augustine, Ponte Vedra, St. Johns, Jacksonville, Daytona Beach and Northeast Florida. Whether your story lives inside an office, studio, restaurant, construction site or storefront, the location becomes part of what we create.

Can you photograph at our office or place of business?

Yes. Photographing you in the place where the work actually happens often creates the strongest images. We plan the spaces, light, people, products and details before the session so the final gallery feels intentional without feeling staged.

Can you photograph our entire team?

Yes. Team and corporate headshot days begin at $1,200. I can create consistent individual headshots, team portraits and candid working images during the same project, giving your company a cohesive library for your website, LinkedIn, recruiting, press and marketing.

What kinds of businesses do you photograph?

I work with founders, realtors, creators, professional teams, designers, builders, wellness businesses, restaurants, retailers and companies launching something new. The industry can change. What matters is that you have built something worth showing people.

How can we use the finished photographs?

Use them across your website, social media, email and marketing. Large-scale advertising such as billboards is licensed separately. The specifics are set out in your agreement, so you always know exactly where you stand.
